Weave Route Planner

Team builder for Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave. Plan each Flame Lord's path, chart every unit's certification route, and compare growth rates side by side in any class.

Data sources. Class growth modifiers, stat bonuses, movement, weapons, masteries and suggested certification skills: Marigold. Personal growth rates: KeenGamer growth table. Recommended class routes and alternatives: KeenGamer best-class guide. Recruitment gates and Part II/III joins: KeenGamer recruitment guide, cross-checked against the chapter timings and Cai-path gates in Marigold's planner. Where the route guide recommends a class the unit cannot take (Orichaldia is Cai-only; Wing Soldier and Celestial Trooper are female-only), the planner flags it instead of hiding it. Steps marked inferred fill gaps the guide left open. Base stats are not included because no complete public table exists yet.

Fan-made tool. Not affiliated with Nintendo or Intelligent Systems.
